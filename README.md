You are a senior product designer and UI/UX architect.

Design a complete, modern, production-ready UI/UX for a multi-business digital commerce platform called:

“YOUR BUSINESS. YOUR STOREFRONT. YOUR CUSTOMERS. ONE PLATFORM.”

The platform allows different types of businesses to create their own online storefront, sell products or services, accept orders/bookings, manage customers, track transactions, build reputation, and grow their business - all from one platform.

IMPORTANT:

This is NOT just a marketplace and NOT just a website builder.

It is a combination of:

1. Individual vendor storefronts

2. Business management dashboard

3. Central payment infrastructure

4. Marketplace/discovery platform

5. Trust and verification system

6. Business growth tools

Do not design it like a generic Shopify clone, generic marketplace, or food-delivery app.

The product should feel like a serious African/Nigerian commerce technology company that could eventually scale internationally.

9. VERIFICATION SYSTEM

==================================================

Trust is a major feature.

Verification is NOT something a vendor can buy.

Premium subscription does NOT equal verification.

The verification journey:

NEW VENDOR

→

MEETS ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

→

APPLY FOR VERIFICATION

→

PLATFORM REVIEW

→

VERIFIED BUSINESS

IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT:

A vendor must successfully serve at least FIVE DIFFERENT CUSTOMERS before they become eligible to apply for verification.

Completing five transactions does NOT automatically verify them.

It only unlocks:

“Apply for Verification”

Other eligibility signals include:

- Successful transactions with different customers

- Acceptable customer satisfaction

- No serious unresolved complaints

- No confirmed fraud

- Required identity/business information

- Compliance with platform policies

Design:

Verification progress card

Example:

“3 of 5 customers successfully served”

Progress bar

“2 more customers needed before you can apply.”

Once eligible:

“Congratulations! You are now eligible to apply for verification.”

Button:

“Apply for Verification”

Then create a verification application flow.

Admin reviews the application.

Approved:

✓ VERIFIED BUSINESS

Rejected:

“Application not approved”

Show reason and future reapplication information.

Verification can later be suspended or removed if serious issues occur.
